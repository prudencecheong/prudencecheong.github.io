Welcome to My Passion Project

This website will tell the story of what my passion is and how it has shaped my life.

🥖Discovery🥖

The first time I discovered baking was during COVID-19, when everyone was stuck at home. My mum decided to start making bread, cakes, and cookies at home just for fun, and I got interested in it immediately after watching her. I started making it twice a week or even daily. I love the feeling when I'm kneading the dough, it feels so satisfying. Seeing the proofing process make the dough so fluffy was unexpected for me at first.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦Support👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

I grew up in a family that is dedicated to cooking. When my dad and our maid were in the kitchen, I would always stand beside them, watching them cook and learning the skills bit by bit. Even though some materials might be expensive, after watching my family and friends eat what I make, I feel satisfied and want to make even more for them. The smell when it comes out of the kitchen is the best part.
