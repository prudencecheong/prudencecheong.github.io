Artisan Sourdough Bread

Inspired by Alexandra's Kitchen: Homemade Sourdough Bread, Step by Step.

🥖 Homemade Sourdough 🥖

Ingredients The Simple Steps
100g Bubbly Starter Mix: Whisk starter and water, then stir in salt and flour to form a sticky ball.
375g Warm Water Rise: Let rest for 30 mins, then do 4 sets of "stretches and folds" every 30 mins.
500g Bread Flour Bulk: Let the dough rise until it increases in volume by about 50%-75%.
12g Fine Sea Salt Proof: Shape the dough, place in a bowl, and chill in the fridge for 12-48 hours.
Rice Flour
(for dusting)		 Bake: Score the top and bake in a Dutch oven at 450°F (covered) for 30 mins.

The Golden Crust

The secret to this golden crust is baking the bread inside a hot Dutch oven. It traps the steam to create a blistered, crispy crust!

A Beautiful Interior

Following the "long cold proof" in the refrigerator makes the inside of the bread light, airy, and full of those beautiful sourdough bubbles.
