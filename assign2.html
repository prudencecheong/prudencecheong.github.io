Despicable Me (2010)

Despicable Me was released in 2010 and is an American animated comedy film directed by Pierre Coffin and Kyle Balda. This movie was produced by Illumination Entertainment and distributed by Universal Pictures. The voice cast includes Steve Carell, Pierre Coffin, Kristen Wiig, Miranda Cosgrove, Dana Gaier, and Julie Andrews.

Gru, a criminal mastermind, adopts three orphans as pawns to carry out the biggest heist in history. His life takes an unexpected turn when the little girls see the evildoer as their potential father.
