Despicable Me 2

The ex-villain Gru is raising his daughters Margo, Edith and Agnes and researching a legitimate jelly and jam business with Dr. Nefarion and the minions. Meanwhile, a laboratory in Arctic is stolen by a powerful aircraft using magnetism. The Anti-Villain league (AVL) agent Lucy Wilde recruits Gru to help her agency discover who has stolen the laboratory and its research. They know that the thief has a store in a mall, and Gru suspects that the owner of the Mexican restaurant Salsa & Salsa, Eduardo Perez, may be the villain El Macho that is presumed dead.

My favorite character from Despicable Me 2 is the Minions Bob because he is innocent, silly, and surprisingly brave, even though he's one of the smallest Minions. His round eyes, the teddy bear that he carries around, and his childlike demeanor make him lovable and feels like a little brother. His loyalty to Gru and his friends is heartwarming, that makes him be my favourite character in the movie.

Facts about the characters in Despicable Me 2

  • Margo - Supports Gru’s new life as a dad, helps set him up on blind dates, and later accepts Lucy Wilde as her stepmother.
  • Gru - Saves Lucy from El Macho, confesses his feelings, and they marry at the end.
  • Bob - He loves hugs, stuffed animals, and making friends with anything small and cute, including animals and other creatures.
  • Stuart - Stuart loves to play the ukulele and sing. He is often seen carrying his ukulele around in movies and shorts.
