🛠The Baking Equipment🛠

✨"Good bread requires patience, but great bread requires the right tools."✨

❗️ Essential Equipment ❗️

Tools Why it's Essential
Kitchen Scale Measures ingredients by weight for accuracy and consistent results.
Spatula Flexible, non-stick, heat resistant and durable, they are used for mixing, folding, spreading, and scraping bowls.
Thermometers Checks internal temperature of baked goods and ensures precise ingredient temperatures.
Mixing Bowls Needed for mixing batters and doughs. Stainless steel is durable, non-reactive, and heats or chills quickly.
Serrated Knife Slices delicate pastries, levels cake layers, and cuts crusty breads.

Specific Measurements

I learned from my family that precision is key. A simple digital scale ensures that every gram of flour is exact, which makes my pastries perfect every time.

The Final Product

There is nothing more satisfying than watching the pastries turn golden brown in the oven. The smell that fills the kitchen is the best thing ever!
